# How to prepare without violating the new-work rule

The participant guide provided in this conversation states: **"Your demo must only highlight the specific features, code, and functionality that your team built during the hackathon"**, **"New Work Only"**, and finalists must use **the provided MongoDB Atlas Sandbox**. Public repositories are required.

Therefore:

- Treat this repository as an **illustrative preparation prototype**. It was authored before/during planning outside the event and is not automatically eligible to be represented as newly built during the competition.
- Obtain written guidance from the organizers about whether pre-existing scaffolding or templates are allowed. If permitted, disclose precisely which parts existed and which parts were built during the official event.
- The safest strategy is to **start a clean public repository during the allowed hacking window**, build your qualifying implementation then, and only showcase features that genuinely originated during the event.
- Use the organizer-provided Atlas Sandbox for the qualifying demo.
- Do not include employer data, code, banking credentials, customer details, licensed feeds, or any asset that your team does not have the right to publish.
- If your team works in banks, use entirely synthetic accounts and risk policies, as this prototype does.

The purpose of this project is learning, rehearsal, architecture validation, and scope planning; it is not a claim of hackathon eligibility.
